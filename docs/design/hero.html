flowctl

Drive Google Flow from the terminal. Consistent characters, and long videos chained frame by frame.
OpenCLIPython stdlib onlyMITAgent skill included
$ flowctl gen "..." --character "Sir Clive"
# clip 1, 8 s
$ flowctl lastframe clip1.mp4
$ flowctl fromframe last1.png "..."
# clip 2 starts from that exact frame
$ flowctl lastframe clip2.mp4
$ flowctl fromframe last2.png "..."
# clip 3 → one 24 s video
